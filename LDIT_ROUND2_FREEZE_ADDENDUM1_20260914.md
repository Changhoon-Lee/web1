# LDIT Round-2 Freeze Addendum 1

Committed before APiCS language-value outcomes were inspected.

Clarification: Grambank nearest-set comparisons use representation-pair-specific common candidate pools. A third unrelated representation cannot remove candidates from a two-representation estimand. The Main×Fine common refinement is not a primary nearest-neighbor representation; it is retained for exact triplet-common ordering where A–B and A–C use identical A∩B∩C primitive support and identical nonempty refinement cells. Primary pooled-vs-Main and pooled-vs-Fine comparisons retain the frozen support rules: pooled grammar >=40 pair-common primitives, every global source group in the grouped representation >=1 pair-common primitive, PHOIBLE finite, and ASJP >=10 common Holman meanings.

No APiCS holdout rule is changed by this addendum.
