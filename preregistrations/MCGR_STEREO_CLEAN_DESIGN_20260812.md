## Frozen theorem targets

The successor result is authoritative only if it proves or sharply limits all targets below.

### T1 — anchored multimode completion theorem

Let disjoint sectors have fixed diagonal PSD blocks `A_a=H_a H_a^T`, where every `H_a` has full column rank. Prove that every PSD completion with these exact diagonal blocks is uniquely

`X = H C H^T`, `H=blockdiag(H_1,...,H_c)`,

where `C` is a block correlation matrix: `C>=0` and `C_aa=I`.

When every `H_a>=0` admits a nonnegative left inverse, prove with explicit left inverses that entrywise nonnegativity and complete positivity are reflected exactly between `X` and `C`. State that these left-inverse hypotheses are sufficient and do not hold for arbitrary nonnegative factors.

### T2 — exhaustive two-sector arbitrary-rank physical cone

For two sectors of internal ranks `p,q`, prove that every anchored PSD completion is parameterized by

`C(K)=[[I_p,K],[K^T,I_q]]`,

with `||K||_2<=1`. Under the nonnegative-left-inverse hypotheses, prove that the complete physical cone is exactly

`K>=0 entrywise` and `||K||_2<=1`.

The CP step must be justified through the classical bipartite-support CP theorem and must not be claimed as a newly discovered graph theorem.

For target coherence `K_*` and Gram matrices `Q_a=H_a^T H_a`, prove the exact formulas

`fracture^2 = 2 (sigma_1-sigma_2)^2 tr(Q_1 K Q_2 K^T)`,

`distortion^2 = 2 tr(Q_1 (K-K_*) Q_2 (K-K_*)^T)`.

Prove that radial attenuation `K=alpha K_*` is the unique minimum-distortion physical completion under a prescribed closure-fracture budget whenever `K_*` is nonzero and feasible.

### T3 — coherence-graph physicality boundary

For rank-one sectors, transfer the classical completely-positive graph characterization exactly to the sector coherence graph. Distinguish:

- support graphs with no odd cycle of length at least five, where DNN and CP coincide;
- long odd-cycle support, where a DNN relaxation can include nonphysical completions.

For the cycle `C_n`, prove the exact edge-coherence optima

`E_CP=n/2`,

`E_DNN=n/2` for even `n`,

`E_DNN=n/[2 cos(pi/n)]` for odd `n`.

Give an exact `C_5` DNN-not-CP witness and an exact local stochastic iteration in which every physical CP completion is stable but the DNN relaxation alone predicts a false instability. Treat Motzkin–Straus, copositive Horn witnesses, CP-graph theory, and cycle Fourier spectra as prior art ingredients rather than standalone novelty.

### T4 — sector-response full-unit-circle theorem

For a stable base update `P_0` that is self-adjoint in an SPD metric `G`, and a sector-coherence perturbation

`P(Delta)=P_0-tau G^{-1} H Sigma Delta H^T`,

prove the exact finite-dimensional response determinant

`det(zI-P(Delta))/det(zI-P_0) = det(I + tau H^T (zI-P_0)^{-1} G^{-1} H Sigma Delta)`

away from poles, including algebraic multiplicities.

Prove that the Krylov span generated by the sector columns is a reducing subspace for the symmetric similarity of `P_0`, so every coherence-dependent eigenvalue lies in that reachable subspace and the orthogonal complement is unchanged.

When the base is Schur stable and the response determinant is nonzero on the unit circle, prove the exact unstable-eigenvalue count by the argument principle/winding number. The determinant lemma, Birman–Schwinger principle, perturbation determinant, Nyquist/argument principle, and Krylov reduction must be mapped as classical ingredients and killed as standalone novelty.

### T5 — complex coherence crossing

Use the inherited exact positive reversible/CP four-state complex motif. Along the fixed-diagonal completely-positive path

`B(gamma)=Diag(diag B)+gamma[B-Diag(diag B)]`, `0<=gamma<=1`,

at frozen step `t=3/2`, prove by exact rational Schur recursion that the first unit-circle loss is a nonreal conjugate pair, not `+1` or `-1`. Isolate the first crossing in an exact rational interval of width below `2e-14` and prove that all preceding Schur margins remain positive.

This result must explicitly kill any `±1`-only sector-coherence phase law.

## Clean Middlebury stereo workflow

### Input and scientific state

Use `skimage.data.stereo_motorcycle()`, which supplies a rectified RGB stereo pair and ground-truth disparity. Preserve the returned arrays and canonical NumPy SHA-256 values. Resize the left and right RGB images to `16 x 16` with anti-aliasing and convert each to standardized grayscale. Resize the disparity map consistently; valid finite positive disparity is used only to define two deterministic anchor correspondences and native metrics.

The state has two view sectors, left and right, each with 256 coordinates. The frozen truth is the concatenated standardized left/right grayscale pair.

Generate observations by applying the same open-boundary Gaussian PSF of sigma `0.80` to each view and adding Gaussian noise of standard deviation `0.006` times truth standard deviation with NumPy `default_rng(20260817)`.

### Mixer and base physics

Build a four-neighbor bilateral graph from the average of the two resized view intensities. Add unit self-mass before forming the degree matrix. Use lazy reversible spatial mixing with `tau=0.31`.

Use the positive nonsymmetric view mixer

`T=[[0.94,0.06],[0.12,0.88]]`

with view metric `C_view=diag(2,1)`. Set

`W=P kron T`, `M=D kron C_view`, `L=D^{-1} kron I_2`, `G=M W^{-1}`.

The compatible base physics contains, separately in each view:

- every PSF row factor;
- open four-neighbor first-difference factors of nominal weight `0.035`;
- coordinate ridge factors of nominal weight `0.030`.

### Two-view, two-mode anchored completion

Choose exactly two left anchor pixels by this data-independent resized-grid rule: `(row,column)=(5,5)` and `(10,10)`. At each anchor, use the resized disparity to map to the nearest valid right coordinate on the same row, clipped to the `16 x 16` grid. If either disparity is invalid, use the nearest valid pixel in raster order; this fallback must be logged.

Let `H_L` and `H_R` be the two-column coordinate-anchor matrices for the left and right sectors. They have nonnegative left inverses and full column rank unless the two mapped right anchors coincide. If the right anchors coincide, the campaign fails without changing anchors.

Freeze target internal coherence

`K_*=[[4/5,1/5],[1/5,4/5]]`.

Use the exhaustive physical path `K(alpha)=alpha K_*`, `0<=alpha<=1`, and anchored completion

`X(alpha)=H C(alpha) H^T`,

`C(alpha)=[[I_2,alpha K_*],[alpha K_*^T,I_2]]`.

Bridge strength is fixed at `eta=3.0`; physics is `B(alpha)=B_base+eta X(alpha)`.

### Deterministic challenge rule

Before any trajectory, independently certify the first `-1` or complex Schur-loss beta threshold of the sealed split endpoint `alpha=0` and full-coherence endpoint `alpha=1`.

If the full endpoint threshold is not at least one percent below the split threshold, the workflow fails. Otherwise freeze

`beta=(beta_split+beta_full)/2`.

At this beta, certify the first alpha crossing on `[0,1]` using both the full 512-dimensional eigenproblem and the four-dimensional sector-response determinant. If no crossing exists or the two routes disagree beyond the residual-certified bracket, fail.

Freeze `alpha_safe=0.9 alpha_star` and `alpha_fail=min(1,1.1 alpha_star)`. Freeze the largest `alpha_repair<alpha_star` certified to achieve spectral radius at most `0.98`. No trajectory-based tuning is allowed.
