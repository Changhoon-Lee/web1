# SCMC T2 positivity-scope amendment

Date: 2026-08-12 (Asia/Singapore)

Design commit: `00c02f7374f67ad4b30a116b5339118f0f560f4c`

This amendment is committed before loading or inspecting the retina pixel array.

## Defect found

The design freeze stated, in full generality, that a zero smallest generalized eigenvalue of the bridge-defect pencil restricted to the mixing-compatible linear subspace is equivalent to survival of an exact positive metric. That equivalence is false when the compatible linear subspace has dimension greater than one: a zero-defect null vector may be sign-changing even though no strictly positive vector lies in the joint nullspace.

An exact three-sector counterexample is frozen in the final certificate. It uses a disconnected bridge quotient whose kernel contains a sign-changing vector in the mixing-compatible subspace, while the positive part of that subspace has strictly positive defect.

## Corrected theorem scope

Let `U` span the full real linear space `ker R`, let `L_bar` be the positive-semidefinite bridge quotient, and let `N` be the positive sector-mass matrix.

The smallest positive generalized eigenvalue of

`(U^T L_bar U, U^T N U)`

is the exact sharp defect constant on the mixing-compatible linear subspace after quotienting its zero-defect kernel.

The following statements are distinct:

1. a zero generalized eigenvalue is equivalent to a nonzero real metric state in `ker R ∩ ker L_bar`;
2. an exact positive metric survives if and only if `ker R ∩ ker L_bar` contains a strictly positive sector vector;
3. when the positive mixing-compatible closure cone is a unique ray generated by `sigma_star>0`, the normalized bridge defect is the explicit Rayleigh quotient of `sigma_star`, and it is zero if and only if that positive metric survives.

Only statement 3 is used for the clean retina prediction if the builder verifies a unique positive ray. If the positive closure cone is not a unique ray, the retina campaign fails rather than silently applying the ray formula.

No other theorem target, workflow operator, continuation path, or gate is changed.
